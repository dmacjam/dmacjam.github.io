<h3 id="research" class="collapsible-header">
<span class="toggle-icon">▶</span> Research
</h3>
<div id="research" class="collapsible-content">
<p>
<b>The problem.</b> Most AI is trained to finish the task: give the answer, write the code, close the ticket. Good collaborators do something harder. They ask, explain, hold back when you are close and step in when you are stuck, so you come out more capable. This is the <i>assistance dilemma</i>, and I train models to handle it with multi-turn reinforcement learning. 
<br><br>
<b>The approach.</b> Post-training with rewards tied to human outcomes, simulated learners and users as RL environments, and benchmarks that measure <i>how</i> a model teaches and collaborates, not just whether it solves the problem. Grounded in how people actually learn: Socratic questioning, scaffolding and <a href="https://ethz-foundation.ch/en/spotlight/uplift-21-jakub-macina/" target="_blank">Productive Failure</a>.
<br><br>
Working on RL for reasoning and human–AI collaboration since 2022.
<b>Selected work</b>
<br>
<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06446" target="_blank">The Assistance Dilemma</a> (2026): learning to teach via multi-turn RL, with the open <a href="https://huggingface.co/eth-nlped/Eduardo-27b" target="_blank">Eduardo-27B</a> model.
<br>
<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15607" target="_blank">TutorRL</a> (EMNLP 2025 oral): aligning LLMs to teach instead of answer, with the open <a href="https://huggingface.co/eth-nlped/TutorRL-7B-think" target="_blank">TutorRL-7B</a> model.
<br>
<a href="https://eth-lre.github.io/mathtutorbench/" target="_blank">MathTutorBench</a> (EMNLP 2025 oral): a benchmark for the open-ended teaching ability of LLM tutors.
<br>
<a href="https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.372/" target="_blank">MathDial</a> (EMNLP 2023): a widely used dataset of teacher–student tutoring dialogues.
<br>
<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.12835" target="_blank">Reasoning models using Socratic questioning</a> (EMNLP 2022): planning and RL (REINFORCE) with rewards to improve GPT-2 reasoning on GSM8K, an early form of what is now called RL from verifiable rewards.
<br><br>
In classrooms: co-founder of <a href="https://www.learnpf.ai/" target="_blank">LearnPF</a>, which brings this research to 40+ schools.
<br><br>
More: <a href="https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2026/06/how-ai-chatbots-become-better-learning-coaches.html" target="_blank">How AI chatbots become better learning coaches</a> (ETH News, 2026).
<br><br>
<b>Earlier work.</b> <a href="https://github.com/vdlm/meetups" target="_blank">Recommender systems and personalization</a> at Exponea and the Slovak University of Technology.
</p>
